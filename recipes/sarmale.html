Sarmale

Description

Sarmale are traditional Romanian cabbage rolls made from minced meat, typically pork, or a blend of pork and beef, mixed with short-grain rice, chopped onions, and seasonings like thyme, dill, and paprika. The filling is wrapped tightly in leaves of fermented sour cabbage (sauerkraut) or tender vine leaves, then arranged in layers in a pot, often alongside smoked pork meat and bay leaves.

The rolls are slow-cooked for hours in a savory broth of tomato paste and water until the cabbage becomes tender and the flavors meld together. Sarmale are traditionally served hot, accompanied by a side of warm mămăligă and sour cream.

Ingredients

  • 1.5-2 kg sour cabbage (whole leaves)
  • 1 kg grounded pork (for example pork shoulder)
  • 200 grams onions
  • 100 grams short-grain rice
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 150 ml lukewarm water
  • 100 ml concentrated tomato paste / tomato puree
  • Optional: smoked pork (pieces of knuckle, ribs, etc.)
  • 1 tablespoon dried thyme (or 4-5 sprigs of fresh thyme)
  • 1 large bunch fresh dill
  • 1/2 tablespoon sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tablespoon sweet paprika
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse the sour cabbage leaves in lukewarm water (repeat if they are too salty) and drain. Cut the leaves into roughly equal pieces (about 12x12 cm) and trim away the hard ribs. Save the trimmed ribs, small leaves, and torn pieces to use later for lining the cooking pot.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the chopped, washed onions along with a pinch of salt, and sauté frequently until softened.Add the washed and drained rice, stirring constantly for 2 to 3 minutes. Mix in the sweet paprika, turn off the heat, and allow the entire mixture to cool completely.
  3. Place the minced pork into a large bowl. Add the cooled onion-and-rice mixture, pepper, dried thyme, chopped fresh dill, and salt to taste (adjusting carefully since the cabbage and optional smoked meat are already salty). Knead the composition thoroughly while gradually pouring in the lukewarm water to ensure the rice cooks properly without drying out the meat.
  4. Place about 1 heaped tablespoon of the filling onto each cabbage leaf piece. Roll the leaves tightly around the filling and tuck the ends inward to form neat, similarly-sized cylinders.
  5. Chop up all the leftover/unused cabbage and place one-third of it on the bottom of the pot. Add dill stalks (or dried dill stems) and cover them with another third of the chopped cabbage. Place the bay leaves and optional whole peppercorns into the pot. Arrange a layer of rolled sarmale and drizzle them with half of the concentrated tomato paste. Continue adding the remaining sarmale, interleaving pieces of smoked pork ribs or knuckle if desired. Top everything with the remaining chopped cabbage and the rest of the tomato paste.
  6. Pour hot water into the pot until it covers the sarmale by about the width of a hand. Place a small plate upside-down on top of the rolls to prevent them from floating or moving during cooking. Cover the pot with a lid and simmer gently on the stove over low heat for 3 hours. Serve hot, typically accompanied by sour cream and mămăligă (polenta).
