  • 1 tablespoon dried thyme (or 4-5 sprigs of fresh thyme)
  • 1 large bunch fresh dill
  • 1/2 tablespoon sweet paprika
  • 1/2 tablespoon sweet paprika
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper
