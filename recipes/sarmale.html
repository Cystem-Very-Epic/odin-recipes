Sarmale

Description

Sarmale are traditional Romanian cabbage rolls made from minced meat, typically pork, or a blend of pork and beef, mixed with short-grain rice, chopped onions, and seasonings like thyme, dill, and paprika. The filling is wrapped tightly in leaves of fermented sour cabbage (sauerkraut) or tender vine leaves, then arranged in layers in a pot, often alongside smoked pork meat and bay leaves.

The rolls are slow-cooked for hours in a savory broth of tomato paste and water until the cabbage becomes tender and the flavors meld together. Sarmale are traditionally served hot, accompanied by a side of warm mămăligă and sour cream.
