Mici With Mustard And Fries

Description

The best romanian food ever. Mici (also known as mititei) are a traditional Romanian grilled dish made from skinless, sausage-shaped rolls of minced meat. The mixture typically combines pork, beef, and sometimes lamb, heavily seasoned with garlic, savory spices like thyme, anise, and paprika, along with sodium bicarbonate to give the meat its signature springy, juicy texture.

They are best paired with mustard and fries.

Ingredients

  • A frozen bag of cut french fries
  • box of mici meat
  • Fry seasonings
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Preheat your grill or grill pan over medium-high heat. Place the mici onto the hot surface, turning them every few minutes so they cook evenly on all sides, form a nice outer crust, and stay juicy inside.
  2. While the mici are grilling, heat the oil in a deep pan or deep fryer. Carefully add the frozen fries in batches and cook until they turn crispy and golden brown.
  3. Remove the fries from the oil, drain off any excess oil, and toss them immediately with your favorite fry seasonings while they are still hot.
  4. Plate the hot mici alongside the seasoned fries and serve immediately with a generous side of yellow mustard.
